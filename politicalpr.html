Case notes

Frequently asked questions

Common questions candidates and parties ask before hiring a political PR agency in India.

01What does a political PR agency actually do?
A political PR agency manages how a politician or party is perceived — handling press and media relations, crisis response, campaign messaging, digital PR and public opinion tracking, so the public narrative stays consistent and on message.
02How is political PR different from election campaign management?
Political PR focuses on perception and communication across media and digital channels. Election campaign management covers the broader operational work of running an election, including booth-level and ground operations.
03What is included in crisis communication for politicians?
Crisis communication typically covers rapid response messaging, media coordination and reputation management during controversies, negative press cycles or misinformation, aimed at protecting credibility and public trust.
04Do political PR agencies work with independent candidates?
Most political PR agencies serve independent candidates, regional parties and national parties alike, tailoring the scope of work to the size and stage of the campaign. It's worth asking a firm directly about their experience with your situation.
05Can political PR cover both digital and traditional media?
Yes. An integrated political PR approach typically spans press and television alongside social media, WhatsApp outreach and digital advertising, so the message stays consistent across every channel voters encounter.
